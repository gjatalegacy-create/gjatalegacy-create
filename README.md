## Research focus

- **Gjata Collapse Law (GCL):** the project-defined constitutional model that delegates and bounds subordinate system roles.
- **Negative knowledge:** failures, exclusions, and rejected paths preserved as operational evidence.
- **Light–Quantum–Shadow:** named ESS-MAI roles for coordination, reasoning, and verdict/persistence under GCL; “Quantum” does not claim quantum hardware or quantum computation.
- **Traceable deterministic reasoning:** explicit transitions, evidence-bound handoffs, and fail-closed gates.
- **Executable prior art:** reproducible POCs that publish experimental success and failure together.

## Evidence boundary

ESS-MAI is an active experimental research prototype, not a production system, security certification, independent scientific validation, or patent-office novelty determination. A separate private ESS-MAI v1.8.9 workspace is outside this disclosure.
